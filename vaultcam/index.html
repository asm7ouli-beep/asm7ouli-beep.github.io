Photos, videos, notes and passwords are protected with AES-256-GCM encryption and an encrypted database — not just a hidden folder.

🎭 Disguise modes

VaultCam hides in plain sight as a calculator, clock, or notes app. Only your secret PIN gesture opens the real vault.

Wrong PIN? The front camera silently snaps a photo of the intruder and logs the time and place of the attempt — stored privately on your device.
