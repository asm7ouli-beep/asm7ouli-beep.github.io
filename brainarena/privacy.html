Brain Arena — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Aoufi Labs ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the Android application Brain Arena (the "App").

The short version: Brain Arena is offline-first. Your scores, streaks, brain profile and settings are stored on your device. We run no servers and hold no user data. Two optional third-party services are involved: Google AdMob (advertising) and Google Play Games Services (optional sign-in for leaderboards and achievements).

1. Information stored on your device

Game results, XP, levels, coins, streaks, brain-profile statistics, achievements progress, and settings are stored locally in the App's private storage. This data is not transmitted to us and is removed when you uninstall the App.

The "Brain Age" and brain-profile radar are playful self-comparison features — they are not medical measurements and make no medical claims.

2. Google Play Games Services (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, Google processes your Play Games profile (gamer tag and avatar) to provide leaderboards and achievement sync. You can play the entire game without signing in. This processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Scores you submit to a leaderboard are visible to other players under your Play Games gamer tag.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads through Google AdMob (interstitial and optional rewarded ads — never in the middle of a game). To serve, cap, and measure ads, AdMob may process device information, IP address, the resettable Advertising ID, and ad interaction data, as described in Google's Advertising Policy. Where required by law (e.g. EEA/UK), a consent dialog is shown before ads are served. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

4. What we do NOT collect

  • No accounts of our own, no names, emails, phone numbers, or contacts
  • No photos, files, microphone, or precise location
  • No data sold to anyone — we host nothing

5. Children

The App is a general-audience game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your rights

Local game data can be erased by uninstalling the App. To manage Play Games data, use your Google account controls. For advertising data, see myadcenter.google.com.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date above.
