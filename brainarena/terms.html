2. What the App is

Brain Arena is a single-player brain-training game: timed exercises across memory, attention, reasoning, numbers and language, with XP, levels, coins, streaks, daily sessions and missions, a brain profile built from your own scores, a Sleep Journal, a Remember This spaced-repetition tool for material you enter yourself, and BrainLab, a library of short educational articles. On Android it can optionally connect to Google Play Games for leaderboards and achievements.

There is no account to create and no server of ours to connect to. Everything you do stays on your device.

4. Not medical, and not a clinical claim

Brain Arena is entertainment and education. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent anything, and nothing in it is medical advice.
  • The Sleep Journal is a self-report diary. Nothing in it is measured by a sensor. Sleep quality, duration, bedtime, wake time, how rested you felt and whether the night was broken are all simply what you typed. Every figure the App shows you — averages, your longest unbroken stretch, comparisons against your scores — is arithmetic on your own entries, and is only as accurate as they are.
  • Scores are not a cognitive assessment. Your brain profile, levels and category scores describe how you performed in these particular games. They are not an IQ measurement, not a clinical test, and not evidence of the presence or absence of any condition.
  • We make no claim that playing improves your cognition in daily life, at work, or in later age. Research on transfer from brain-training games to real-world ability is mixed, and the App does not pretend otherwise. BrainLab cites published work and says where the evidence is weak.
  • Never use the App in place of professional advice. If you are worried about your memory, concentration, mood or sleep, speak to a doctor. If anything is urgent, contact a medical service — not an app.

If you have a condition affected by flashing visuals, timed pressure or screen use, check with a clinician before playing. Stop and rest if you feel unwell, strained or dizzy.

5. Your data stays on your device — and can be lost

We run no servers. Your progress, statistics, brain profile, Sleep Journal entries and Remember This sets are stored only on your device, and never sent to us.

The consequence matters: if you uninstall the App, clear its data, or lose, reset or replace your device, all of it is gone permanently. There is no cloud save, no account to sign back into, and no copy anywhere for us to restore — we have never held one. Switching between Android and iOS does not carry your progress across.

On Android, if you sign in to Google Play Games, your leaderboard scores and achievements are held by Google and survive a reinstall. Nothing else does.

You are responsible for what you put there. Do not store anyone else's confidential information in Remember This, and remember that the App's data is only as protected as your device's own lock screen.

We have no push server and never send you anything over the internet. They are all off until you switch them on, and you can turn them off in the App or in your device settings. A local reminder can fail to appear if the device is off or restricting background activity; they are a convenience, not a guarantee.

Sharing a results card uses your device's share sheet and happens only when you choose it. Where it goes afterwards is governed by whichever app you sent it to.
