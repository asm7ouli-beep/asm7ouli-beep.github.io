Last updated: 14 November 2026

App: Board Vision Package: aoufi.riadh.boardvisiongame Android 8.0+ · iOS 14+ Publisher: Riadh Aoufi

2. What the App is

Board Vision is a single-player logic puzzle played on a chess board. Squares are shown as attacked, and you deduce which hidden pieces attack them, using real chess movement rules and real blocking. It has a chapter-based curriculum, Zen and Ranked modes, a daily challenge, concept cards that explain each piece, and — on Android — optional Google Play Games leaderboards and achievements.

It teaches genuine chess geometry, but it is a deduction game, not a chess program: you do not play games of chess in it, and it contains no chess engine, opponent or analysis tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to FIDE or any chess federation, club, or any other chess product.

There is no account to create and no server of ours to connect to.

  • Use the App on a device you own or are authorised to use.

4. Your progress stays on your device — and can be lost

We run no servers. Chapter progress, stars, scores, streaks, settings and unlocks are stored only on your device.

If you uninstall the App, clear its data, or lose, reset or replace your device, all of it is gone permanently. There is no cloud save, no account to sign back into and no copy for us to restore. On Android, if you sign in to Google Play Games, your leaderboard scores and achievements are held by Google and survive a reinstall; your chapter progress does not.

5. Hints and rewarded ads

A hint reveals which piece attacks one highlighted square. Hints are limited per puzzle, and in Ranked mode using them affects your score.

  • Watching a rewarded ad is always your choice. Declining costs you nothing but the hint.
  • A hint is granted when the ad network confirms the ad was completed. Ads can fail to load, be unavailable in your region, or be interrupted — in that case the hint may be unavailable, and we cannot credit one manually.
  • Where the App currently grants hints without an ad — for example in the first chapter, or when no ad is available — that is a convenience we may change in a later version. It is not an entitlement.
  • Do not block, spoof or automate ads, or manipulate rewarded-ad completions.

We do not control which ads appear, and any offer inside one is between you and that advertiser.
