Board Vision — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Aoufi Labs ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the application Board Vision (the "App"), published as "Board Vision: Chess Puzzle" on Google Play and "Board Vision Chess" on the App Store.

The short version: Board Vision is a single-player puzzle game. It has no accounts and does not collect your name, email, or contacts. Your progress and settings live only on your device. The one thing that involves data is in-app advertising (Google AdMob), which uses device and advertising identifiers to show and measure ads. You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings at any time.

1. Information stored on your device

The App saves gameplay data locally in its private storage on your device — chapter progress, stars, daily-challenge streaks, and your settings (sound, theme, accessibility). This data stays on your device, is not transmitted to us, and is removed when you uninstall the App.

2. Information collected through advertising

The App displays ads through Google AdMob. To serve, cap, and measure ads, AdMob may process:

  • Device information (model, OS version, language, screen size)
  • IP address (used to approximate general location)
  • Advertising identifier (the resettable Advertising ID on Android, or the IDFA on iOS if you allow tracking)
  • Ad interaction data (views, clicks)

This processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Advertising Policy. Where required by law (e.g. EEA/UK), you will be shown a consent dialog before ads are served.

  • Android: reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • iOS: the App asks permission before any tracking, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. Declining is fine: ads still work, they are simply not personalized. Change your answer any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

You can review or change your ad consent choices at any time from Settings → Privacy choices inside the App, where that option applies to your region.

3. Leaderboards and notifications

Android: if you sign in with Google Play Games, Google processes your Play Games profile (gamer tag and avatar) to provide leaderboards and achievements. You can play the entire game without signing in. iOS: Board Vision has no leaderboards, so no score ever leaves your iPhone.

The optional daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your own device. The App has no push server and sends you nothing from the internet. Turn it off any time in the App's settings or your device's notification settings.

4. What we do NOT collect

  • No accounts, names, emails, phone numbers, or contacts
  • No photos, files, or precise location
  • No data sold to anyone — we run no servers and hold no user data

5. Children

The App is a general-audience logic game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your rights

Your gameplay data can be erased at any time by uninstalling the App. For data processed by Google for advertising, use Google's controls at myadcenter.google.com.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date above.
